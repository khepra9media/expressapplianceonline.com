Duct Cleaning & Repair you can count on

Leaky or dirty ductwork makes a good furnace or AC work harder for less comfort. We clean supply and return ducts, seal leaking joints, and repair or replace damaged runs so the air actually reaches the rooms it is meant to.

Dryer vent cleaning is part of this service. A lint-packed dryer vent is one of the most common causes of house fires, and it is a quick job to clear.

Common problems we fix

  • Excess dust or musty smell when the system runs
  • One room much hotter or colder than the rest
  • Weak airflow from vents
  • Ducts disconnected or crushed in the basement or attic
  • Leaking joints wasting heated or cooled air
  • Rodent or pest activity in ducts
  • Dryer vent duct clogged with lint
